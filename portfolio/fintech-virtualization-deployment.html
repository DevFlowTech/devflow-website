64-Node Dell Virtualization Cluster Setup

Project Overview

A prominent fintech company operating in the GIFT City Gandhinagar special economic zone required a high-availability server cluster capable of hosting virtualized environments with absolute zero downtime.

Technical Challenge

The platform needed to run secure financial transactions, processing thousands of requests per second. The client had no local technical operations staff in Gujarat and needed pre-configured hardware systems delivered, mounted, wired, and integrated into their private cloud infrastructure with full monitoring capabilities.

The Solution

Vassu Infotech configured and deployed a **64-Node Dell PowerEdge cluster** mounted in high-efficiency cooling racks:

  • Processor configuration: Dual Intel Xeon Gold per node, totaling 1,024 physical computing cores.
  • Storage configuration: High-speed NVMe flash array configured under hardware RAID 10 to ensure zero write bottleneck.
  • Hypervisor Setup: Pre-configured VMware ESXi hypervisors with high-availability failover groups.
  • SLA Monitoring: Integrating Zabbix agents and active ping scripts reporting directly to Vassu Infotech's support center in Ahmedabad.

Key Metric Achieved

The virtualization cluster was installed, configured, and integrated with the client's internal network within **48 hours**, resulting in a stable environment that has maintained **100% uptime** since deployment.

Why the Client Chose Vassu Infotech

The fintech firm evaluated multiple infrastructure vendors in Gujarat before selecting Vassu Infotech. Key deciding factors included our pre-configured delivery model, genuine Dell PowerEdge hardware sourcing, on-site rack integration in GIFT City, and the legally binding 2-hour hardware replacement SLA covering the entire cluster lifecycle.

Hardware & Software Stack Delivered

  • Compute Nodes: 64x Dell PowerEdge R740/R750 2U rack servers with dual Intel Xeon Gold processors.
  • Memory: 768 GB registered ECC RAM per node for high-concurrency transaction workloads.
  • Storage: Enterprise NVMe flash arrays configured under hardware RAID 10 with hot-swap redundancy.
  • Virtualization: VMware ESXi 7 with vCenter high-availability failover groups.
  • Monitoring: Zabbix agents with active ping scripts reporting to our Ahmedabad NOC 24/7.
  • Networking: Redundant 10GbE switching with private subnet isolation for financial data.

Results & Business Impact

  • Zero downtime since production cutover, meeting the client's 99.99% availability target.
  • 1,024 physical compute cores provisioned for a 3x growth in transaction processing capacity.
  • Remote management (iDRAC Enterprise) reduced on-site admin visits by over 70%.
  • Monthly AMC plan with 2-hour on-site response across GIFT City and Ahmedabad.
